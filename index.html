Three ways to open the Calea website

Anna, here are three directions for the Calea homepage. All three use the Calea logo, the two brand colors, the request-to-book approach we agreed on, and the Francis Bay webcam you asked about. What changes between them is how the house is presented.

Please don't judge the photographs. Most of them are my site-visit shots from September 6, taken before the interiors were finished. Kayden's October and December photos will replace them. For now, focus on the layout, the pace and the feel.

Option A

Immersive

The house is the argument. Photographs run edge to edge, the menu steps aside until you scroll, and each section says one thing.

Choose it ifYou want the first impression to be the view and the architecture, with nothing in between.
