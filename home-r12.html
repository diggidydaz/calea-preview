Living spaces · Indoor

Room to Gather

At the heart of Calea, the main living room opens to sweeping panoramic views and the lush landscape of Francis Bay.

Expansive sliding doors dissolve the boundary between indoors and out, connecting the room to the pool terrace and bringing the island’s natural beauty into everyday life.

Generous seating, thoughtfully selected furnishings, and understated technology make this space equally suited to quiet mornings, afternoon conversation, and evening gatherings.

The rooftop at sunset

Crescentia cujete

The calabash is a remarkable island tree, its delicate flowers and great green fruits emerging directly from its trunk and branches. The sturdy fruits mature into hard, hollow vessels, transformed by generations of island hands into bowls, cups, musical instruments and works of art.
